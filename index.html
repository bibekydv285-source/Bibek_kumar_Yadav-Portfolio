How I work

From first idea to a live website

A simple four-step process, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Step 01

    Discover

    We talk through your goals, audience and content so the site solves the right problem.

  2. Step 02

    Design

    I plan the pages and a clean, mobile-first layout that matches your brand.

  3. Step 03

    Build

    I code the site or theme, connect forms, bookings and WhatsApp, and test it on every screen.

  4. Step 04

    Launch

    I set up the domain, DNS and hosting, go live, and stay on hand for updates.
