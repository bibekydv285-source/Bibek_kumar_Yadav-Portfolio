FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you deliver to?

We currently deliver to Kathmandu, Bhaktapur, Lalitpur, and Duwakot. Contact us on WhatsApp to confirm your specific area.

Can I order multiple products at once?

Yes! Use our Order section to select multiple items like milk, ghee, paneer, and curd all in one order — or use the Quick Add dropdown to add products by name.

Is the milk really fresh and pure?

Absolutely! Our milk is collected fresh from our partner farms every morning at 4 AM and delivered directly without any preservatives or added water.

What are your delivery timings?

We offer morning delivery (5-8 AM), afternoon (12-2 PM), and evening (5-7 PM). For morning delivery, please order before 8 PM the previous evening.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery, as well as digital payments through eSewa, Khalti, and bank transfer. No advance payment required for regular customers.

Do you offer monthly subscription plans?

Yes! We have flexible monthly subscription plans with additional discounts. Contact us on WhatsApp to set up your personalized subscription.
