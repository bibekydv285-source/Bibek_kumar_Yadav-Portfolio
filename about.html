About

Home | About Me

Hello! My name is Bibek Kumar Yadav. I hail from Chhinnamasta Rural Municipality-4, Saptari, but I currently reside in Kathmandu. I'm an undergraduate student at ISMT College, affiliated with the University of Sunderland. I'm a self-motivated, dedicated, and highly focused individual, and my goal is to establish myself as a Full Stack Developer and Security Specialist, building scalable, modern web applications using the MERN stack and cutting-edge technologies.

My Story:

I began my journey in tech by building websites using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, which helped me understand how design and logic come together to create meaningful digital experiences.

Over time, I expanded beyond front-end development and now work across the full MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React, Node.js), building scalable, dynamic, and user-focused web applications.

I also develop cross-platform mobile applications using Flutter, allowing me to deliver smooth and consistent experiences on both Android and iOS. On the backend and systems side, I work with C# for structured, object-oriented development and Python for scripting, automation, and problem-solving.

Alongside development, I actively work on transcription projects, handling accuracy, formatting, and clarity with strong attention to detail.

I regularly practice coding challenges and problem-solving, strengthening my logical thinking, algorithmic skills, and ability to write clean, efficient code.

Quality is my top priority whether its structuring a backend API, designing a responsive UI, writing optimized queries, or delivering precise transcriptions.

Today, I focus on creating complete digital solutions that blend performance, functionality, and usability.

I'm always open to new challenges and collaborations if you have an idea, a project, or a problem to solve, lets work together and build something impactful.

Bibek.
